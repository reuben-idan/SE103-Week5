What * meant in my pattern:
The * matched any sequence of characters in a filename. In data*.txt, it matched the numbers in data1.txt, data2.txt, and data3.txt.

Why I listed matches before copying:
I listed the matching files first so I could verify exactly which files the wildcard selected. Checking the list before copying helps avoid including unintended files.

Why wildcards are dangerous with delete commands:
A wildcard can match more files than expected, and a delete command may remove all of those matches immediately. If the pattern is too broad or run from the wrong directory, important files could be lost. I should inspect the matches and current directory before any destructive command.
